SQUADRON OPS v2 — 10-MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT
================================================================================
Audience: SDO, maintainer, or senior aviator
Prerequisite: reseed (python seed.py), servers running on :5174 / :8001

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MINUTE 0–1 | DASHBOARD — SITUATIONAL AWARENESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open: http://localhost:5174/

Talking points:
  - "Morning brief" view: personnel, aircraft readiness, currency warnings
  - Computed vs stamped: computed reflects open discrepancies and inspections;
    stamped is what the line has released safe for flight
  - Next 24 Hours: today's scheduled sorties

Click: Maintenance link if drift banner visible (expect 3 aircraft after reseed)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MINUTE 1–3 | MAINTENANCE — STAMPED VS COMPUTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open: /maintenance

Talking points:
  - Stat strip uses computed FMC/PMC/NMC (not stamped)
  - Yellow banner: aircraft awaiting QA release
  - 614: stamped FMC, computed PMC — flyable with degraded capability
  - 615: DOWNING in work — not safe for flight until resolved
  - 617: DOWNING awaiting parts (AWP) — computed NMCS

If all green (no drift): reseed before demo.

Click: 614 → aircraft maintenance detail

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MINUTE 3–5 | QA RELEASE — CLOSE THE MAINTAINER LOOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open: /maintenance/{614-id}

Talking points:
  - Inspections with overdue/downing flags
  - Open discrepancies with MAF numbers and work status
  - QA Release card: after QA signoff, stamp line status to computed

Action (614):
  1. Open QA Release
  2. Enter QA notes: "QA inspection complete; FLIR degraded ops authorized IAW NATOPS"
  3. Release for Flight
  4. Toast confirms status sync; stamped now PMC

Optional (615 or 617): show release blocked — not safe for flight

Return: Dashboard — drift count decreased

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MINUTE 5–8 | SORTIE COMPLETION — CASCADE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open: /sorties → pick incomplete sortie (prefer ASU/NVG P211 on day+2)

Talking points:
  - Schedule assigned crew; Complete Sortie logs actuals
  - Per-crew night/NVG/instrument hours; Apply mission profile to all crew
  - Validation prevents incomplete times or missing crew hours

Action:
  1. Complete Sortie
  2. Confirm takeoff/landing times, crew hours
  3. Submit

Open: /crew/{HAC-id} — show NIGHT_NVD (or applicable) currency renewed

Talking points:
  - Sortie completion cascades to currencies, aircraft hours, training jacket
  - One system, not three spreadsheets

Optional: complete SIM_TOFT P200 sortie — note sim-ineligible currencies skip

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MINUTE 8–10 | TRAINING + BOARDS (OPTIONAL CLOSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open: /training — syllabus and gradecards

Open: /board → Squadron Snapshot (TV mode)
  - Clarify: operational snapshot, not WTM T-ratings yet

Open: /admin → Audit tab
  - QA release and sortie completion logged

Closing line:
  "This is the spine — fly, log, readiness updates, maintenance reconciles.
   WTM T-ratings and full 4790 chain are on the roadmap."
